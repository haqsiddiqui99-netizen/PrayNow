Privacy

PrayNow is a prayer-times and mosque app. This page says what the app stores and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Your name, mobile number, and a hashed password, when you create an account.
  • The mosques you follow, your notification token, and messages sent to you.
  • Mosque details, photos, and corrections you submit.
  • Questions you send to the in-app guide. Those are sent to OpenAI to produce an answer. A spoken question is sent to OpenAI to turn it into text, and the audio is not kept.
  • For mosque admins: azan recordings and voice announcements you upload. Live azan audio passes through Agora while it plays and is not recorded unless you save a recording.

Services we use

To show the weather and air quality, the app sends your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to Open-Meteo. It does not include your name or account. Push notifications go through Google Firebase and Expo.

What stays on your phone

Your location is used on the phone to find nearby mosques, prayer times, and the Qibla direction. It is not saved as a location history on our server. Prayer tracking, tasbih, and Quran progress are stored on the phone.

Who can see it

Mosque managers see corrections and requests for their own mosque. We do not sell personal information.

Delete your account

In the app, open the account menu and choose Delete account, then enter your password. You can also do it on the web at the delete account page. Deleting removes your account, follows, notification token, and messages. Mosque records you created stay, with your name removed from them.
